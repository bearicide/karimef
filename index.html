Repair Flow

Simple visitor-facing explanation: look, test, trace, repair, document.

Look

Check symptoms, ports, buttons, power behavior, dirt, damage, and obvious clues.

Test

Check power, heat, signal, loose parts, and repeatable failure patterns.

Fix + Document

Repair what can be repaired, explain what happened, and keep notes for the next person.
