Directed Process

Professional enough for field colleagues, clear enough for clients, and structured enough to become publishable technical content.

01

Observe. Capture the actual failure state before disassembly, including environment, accessory variables, user sequence, and repeatability.

02

Isolate. Separate mechanical, electrical, firmware-adjacent, connector, power, and user-interface causes through controlled tests.

03

Intervene. Repair or stabilize using the least destructive path first, preserving original condition and documenting irreversible steps.

04

Validate. Confirm post-repair behavior with stress, continuity, charge/discharge, repeat-cycle, and handling tests as appropriate.
